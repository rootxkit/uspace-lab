# 08 — Open questions for the ministry and GCAA

Each question states what the spec assumes until answered and which parts of the design it changes.

| # | Question | Assumed for now | Affects |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Is Reg. (EU) 2021/664 (with 665 and 666) adopted or planned in Georgian law under the Common Aviation Area Agreement, and on what timeline? | Not yet adopted; the 2019/947-equivalent GCAA rules of 2021-01-01 apply nationally; the system is built so the 2021/664 layer activates per designated volume | whether USSP certification, CISP designation and mandatory services have legal force; `01` |
| Q2 | Will U-space airspace be designated, where (Tbilisi CTR, corridors, ports), and with which services mandatory (is conformance monitoring mandatory)? | None designated; `uspace_airspaces` empty; conformance treated as mandatory in the design | `01 §3`, `03`, authorisation gating in `02 F5` |
| Q3 | Who is the ANSP counterpart (Sakaeronavigatsia) and what is its role: dynamic reconfiguration only, or also a surveillance feed to U-space? Which surveillance data may leave the ANSP (ADS-B only, or ATM track data) and under what agreement? | ANSP does dynamic restrictions and provides a position feed for aircraft near U-space airspace; ADS-B class data | `02 F2, F4`, `uspace-ansp` scope, trust class of manned tracks |
| Q4 | Can the new authority system become the registry of record, or must it mirror uas.gov.ge? If mirror: what export or API exists, in what format and cadence, and under which data-sharing agreement? | Mirror by periodic import until the authority system is adopted; manual entry before that | `03 §1`, A-M1, PII handling in `06` |
| Q5 | What is the operator registration number format, and does Georgia adopt the EU AMC form (16 characters: 3 upper-case country letters, 12 lower-case alphanumerics, 1 checksum; plus 3 secret characters — AMC1 Article 14(6) of 2019/947, not re-read)? Is the verification code broadcast or only checked at upload? | EU-like public part; secret part optional | identification matching (`04 §3.2`), broadcast comparison, hashing in `06` |
| Q6 | In which format will GCAA provide zones: ED-318 JSON, an ArcGIS/airspace.gov.ge export with a rules file, AIXM, or manual authoring in the new console? Who owns the vertical limits and schedules that airspace.gov.ge does not publish? | Authored in the authority console, with an airspace.gov.ge import needing a rules file from GCAA | A-M1, `02 F1` |
| Q7 | **Decided: the single CISP is state-run (`uspace-cisp`)**, certified under Art. 5(7). Still open: who operates the DSS (no regulation assigns it) and who is the ecosystem token issuer; and whether GCAA adopts the conformance suite of `00 §7` as a certification condition for third-party USSPs and CISPs | DSS hosted alongside the CISP, token issuer at the authority, suite proposed as a certification condition — national choices | `01 §2`, `02 F6`, `06`, deployment |
| Q8 | Data retention and protection rules above the regulatory floor (30 days for USSP/CISP operational records, 2021/664 Art. 15(1)(g); 24 h for F3411 DP and F3548 peer caches): how long must telemetry, records, audit and incident data be kept, what does the authority require under Art. 18(b), and what are the lawful bases for police access to operator identity under the Law of Georgia on Personal Data Protection? | 90 days telemetry online, 2 years archive, 5 years alerts/intents, incidents indefinite, audit 10 years; DPIA before go-live | `05 §4`, `06 §5`, F10 |
| Q9 | Occurrence reporting: is Reg. 376/2014 (with 2015/1018 for UAS occurrence classes) or an equivalent in force, is ECCAIRS/ADREP (E5X) the target format, and is GCAA the designated independent authority of Art. 6(3) and the single national intake? | Authority is the intake; E5X export built when confirmed; reporter protection of Art. 15–16 applied regardless | A-M3, `02 F7` |
| Q17 | Which Art. 3(4) requirements will the designation set per U-space airspace (UAS capabilities, service update frequencies and latencies, operational conditions incl. any height ceiling), and is a published airspace risk assessment available? | 1 Hz network ID and traffic information, 1 s CIS notification target, conformance monitoring required | `01` A3, A11; `02`; `05 §7` |
| Q18 | Which e-conspicuity devices and which receiver (USSP-owned or contracted) satisfy SERA.6005(c) for U-space airspace outside ATC service? | ADS-B 1090 MHz receiver run by the USSP; ADS-L deferred | `02 F4`, S-M3 |
| Q10 | Which terrain model may be used for AGL judgements (Copernicus GLO-30, SRTM, a national DEM), and which geoid (EGM2008)? | Copernicus GLO-30 and EGM2008 2.5' | 120 m detection, AGL zone limits |
| Q11 | Direct Remote ID: which receivers will GCAA deploy (count, sites, ownership), and is there a legal basis for police or municipalities to operate receivers that feed the authority? | A small GCAA-owned fleet first; third-party receivers as separate instances with their own keys | `02 F9`, A-M2 |
| Q12 | Priority rules beyond the regulation (2021/664 Art. 10(8)–(9) fixes priority for SERA Art. 4 special operations, then first come first served): any national priority classes, and the specific-category authorisation workflow — does the USSP escalate to the authority inside the system, or reference an authorisation issued outside it? | Reference an externally issued authorisation id; only the Art. 10(8) priority class in the F3548 `priority` field | S-M1 |
| Q13 | Will there be more than one USSP in Georgia in the planning horizon, and may courier (an operator) also become a USSP client of a different USSP? | One USSP (`uspace-ussp`) initially; DSS built in from day one | S-M4 |
| Q14 | Manned e-conspicuity (2021/666): which devices will Georgian GA carry in U-space airspace (ADS-B, ADS-L, FLARM), so the ANSP or the USSP knows what to receive? | ADS-B 1090 MHz via the ANSP; ADS-L deferred | `02 F4` |
| Q15 | Languages and accessibility obligations for public interfaces (`ka`, `en`, others) and official branding rules for state systems. | `ka` and `en` from day one; branding as configuration | consoles and portals |
| Q16 | Hosting: when do production domains and state hosting take over from `*.chikox.net`, and what certification or security assessment does state hosting require? | Staging on one droplet until the authority's A-M5 | `05 §6`, `06 §4` |
